Prologue — The Longest Night

It was nearly two in the morning when Aarav realized sleep was not coming back. The silence inside his room felt heavier than usual, as if the air itself carried the weight of unanswered questions.

He sat on the edge of his bed, staring at the dim light coming from his phone screen. The last message he had sent hours ago remained unread. A simple message — "Are you okay?" — yet it held every emotion he was too afraid to express.

He refreshed the screen again and again, hoping to see her name appear. Hoping to hear the soft vibration of a reply. But nothing happened.

That night stretched endlessly, and in that silence, he slowly understood something painful — sometimes life changes without warning, and sometimes love ends without explanation.

"Are you really going to be okay?" his friend once asked quietly, noticing the tired look in his eyes.

Aarav forced a small smile and replied, "I don't know yet… but I will be. I have to be."

Those simple words were not just an answer. They were a promise he made to himself — a promise to keep moving forward, no matter how heavy life felt.

Chapter 15 — The Good Ending

On a quiet morning, Aarav stood beside his parents, smiling as they prepared for a small family celebration. The house was filled with laughter, warmth, and the comforting smell of home-cooked food.

He looked around and saw the people who had supported him through his darkest days. Their presence reminded him that love never truly disappears — it simply changes form.

Later that night, standing alone under the open sky, he took a deep breath and closed his eyes.

He remembered the boy he once was — the boy who stayed awake at two in the morning, waiting for a message that never came.

That boy had grown into a man who understood life, accepted loss, and found peace within himself.

There were still memories. There were still scars. But there was also strength, gratitude, and hope.

And in that peaceful silence, Aarav smiled — not because everything was perfect, but because he had survived, healed, and learned to live again.

That was his good ending — not a fairy-tale ending, not a perfect ending, but a real one. A life built from pain, patience, and courage. He had learned that losing someone does not mean losing yourself, and sometimes the strongest hearts are the ones that survive the quietest battles. Standing there under the open sky, he felt something he had not felt in years — peace, deep and steady, like the calm after a long storm.
